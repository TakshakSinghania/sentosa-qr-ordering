We built the complete tabletop ordering and kitchen dispatch system for Sentosa — The Coffee Unit.

Unlike generic self-ordering kiosks, Sentosa preserves the quiet craft and tactile hospitality of a specialty coffee house:
• Instant Table 05 QR resolution & table affinity recommendations (“Guests here often order…”)
• Tactile modifier customization with authoritative real-time cart pricing
• Frictionless 6-digit phone OTP authentication with persistent order tracking
• Real-time WebSocket kitchen dispatch (KDS) & table waiter calls

Engineered with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, WebSockets, PostgreSQL, and Prisma.
